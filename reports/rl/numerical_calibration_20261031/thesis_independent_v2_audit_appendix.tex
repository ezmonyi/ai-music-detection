\section{Independent audit of the reviewed receiver protocol}
\label{sec:reviewed-receiver-v2}
The failed initial cross-GPU budget study was retained unchanged. After reviewing
its discovery observations, the experiment owner explicitly approved a new,
receiver-specific absolute log-density budget of $3\times10^{-4}$, conditional on
an independent audit. The budget and selection were fixed before this audit;
the earlier audit was not reused to validate a post hoc threshold.

Twenty-four new training prompt groups comprised twelve RTX5060/group-100
behavior groups and twelve A6000/group-10 behavior groups. Four candidates and
all 31 stochastic transitions yielded 2,976 observations. Their median, 95th
percentile, 99th percentile and maximum absolute log-density errors were
$9.05991\times10^{-6}$, $3.96371\times10^{-5}$,
$6.06775\times10^{-5}$ and $1.02997\times10^{-4}$, respectively. All observations
passed the reviewed budget, whereas only 183 passed the original $10^{-6}$
budget. Repeated forwards and the two native 31-step controls had zero measured
discrepancy, and corrupted-probability negative controls were rejected.

These are dimension-mean Gaussian log densities. The reviewed budget bounds
the multiplicative mean-density surrogate-ratio discrepancy by
$\exp(3\times10^{-4})-1\simeq0.0300045\%$; the observed maximum corresponds to
$0.0103002\%$. This does not guarantee exact joint-trajectory importance
sampling or arbitrary cross-GPU equivalence. Original cached probabilities,
FP32 trajectories, BF16 backbone, FP32 adapters, sampler, reward and quality
guards were unchanged, and native/repeat controls retained $10^{-6}$.

The explicit receiver wrapper preserves the original strict default and binds
the reviewed allowance to raw audit evidence, authorization, receiver flags and
model/runtime identities. The original scientific core remains identical to
commit \texttt{1480889}; independent-audit and receiver-wrapper additions are
commits \texttt{3fd46e8} and \texttt{8857c3e}. This zero-update audit establishes
numerical admission only. Completion of 1,000 additional real optimizer updates,
adequate valid training groups and any fixed-test audio improvement require
separate evidence.
