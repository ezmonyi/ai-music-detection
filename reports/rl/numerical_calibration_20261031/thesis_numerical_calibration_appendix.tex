\section{Independent cross-GPU likelihood calibration}
\label{sec:cross-gpu-calibration}
A separate zero-update study tested frozen ACE-Step flow policies on a receiver RTX3090, with a BF16 backbone and lossless FP32 trajectories. A pre-registered split used 48 training prompt groups, excluding the prior discovery observations and all validation/test identities. Four candidates and eight stochastic times yielded 768 calibration and768 independent-audit observations. Both native policies reproduced31 stochastic transitions exactly on the staged and regular backends, and repeated forwards in all 1,536 observations had zero measured difference.

The maximum absolute log-density discrepancy was $9.60827\times10^{-5}$ in calibration and $1.16467\times10^{-4}$ in audit; only 92 of 1,536 observations satisfied the original $10^{-6}$ threshold. The study used dimension-mean Gaussian log densities, so the observed audit maximum corresponds to a surrogate-ratio uncertainty bound of approximately $0.0116474\%$. This is not an exact joint-trajectory importance-sampling guarantee. The pre-registered $0.01\%$ ceiling and twofold calibration safety margin produced no admissible replacement budget. The negative controls passed, but no production threshold was activated and no optimizer step was taken.

A post hoc $3\times10^{-4}$ receiver-specific budget is documented only as a proposal requiring new independent audit and review. The original audit cannot validate a threshold chosen after observing it. Matched-seed fixed-prompt comparisons between a future offline-RL checkpoint and the official checkpoint remain pending; no new RL checkpoint or heldout audio was produced in this study.
