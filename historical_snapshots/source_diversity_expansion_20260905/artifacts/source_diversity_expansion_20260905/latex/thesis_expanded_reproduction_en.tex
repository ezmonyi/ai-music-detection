The formal evaluation directories retain raw source-pair metrics, all quantity
and combination summaries, actual training counts, fold membership, frozen
candidate/model files, and record-level locked scores. The full feature-status
tables distinguish natural unobservability from processing failure. The raw
Beat This exception probes are preserved in
\filepath{results/beat_raw_probe_locked_audiox.json} and its paired NumPy archive.
These records prevent an empty or failed estimator output from being silently
reinterpreted as perfectly regular Human or AI music.
The checkpoint manifest records the actual eight ordered Harmonix ensemble
weights, the resolved Demucs signature, and Beat This checkpoint hashes, rather
than relying only on model aliases. A separate input-duration retry incident,
its quarantined output and guarded exact-thirty-second re-run are documented in
\filepath{results/beat_retry_duration_incident_30s.json}. The wrong-duration
output was excluded before feature-union completion and classifier fitting.

Code and metadata are portable; absolute remote waveform paths require access
to the retained storage. Lightweight CSVs and accounting manifests are mirrored
locally and their positive union verifications regenerated for the local paths
before CPU-only fitting. The archive index excludes transient PDF previews and
synthetic runtime benchmarks from the scientific result index. The preceding
thesis revision remains unchanged, preserving the chronology of hypotheses,
external measurement failures, frozen tests and later development reuse.
