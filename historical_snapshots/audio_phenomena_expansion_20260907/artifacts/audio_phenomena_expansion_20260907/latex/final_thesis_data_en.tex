\section{Data provenance and analysis populations}
\label{sec:final-data}

The project uses a dated recording registry, several overlapping analysis
cohorts, and separate measurement-validation corpora. Their counts answer
different questions. They must not be summed into a single number of independent
songs, and external phenomenon-validation recordings are not automatically
members of the AI/Human classification population.

\subsection{Frozen expanded registry}

The September 5 registry contains 10,141 selected recording identities:
3,091 Human and 7,050 AI-labelled. It has 10,140 distinct retained-native byte
hashes and 3,979 global split groups. The duplicate MoisesDB pair shares a
group and training weight. Exact-ten-second views cover the registry; 4,497
recordings satisfy the actual-duration condition for thirty-second analysis.
Eligible development populations contain 8,361 and 3,317 rows, respectively.
Locked, stress, provisional, and pilot roles are not silently admitted to
development fitting.

The AI-labelled registry contains 500 entries each for ACE-Step, AudioLDM 2
Large, AudioLDM 2 Music, HeartMuLa, MusicGen Large, MusicGen Medium, MusicGen
Small, Mustango, Riffusion, Stable Audio v1, Stable Audio v2, Suno, Udio, and
provisional AudioX. DiffRhythm contributes 50 files but only ten conditioning
groups. AudioX is not verified AI ground truth and receives no accuracy claim.
Model-size or version variants are not assumed to be independent generator
families. Short excerpts from some sources cannot support the long-window
analyses merely because 500 files are available.

All 2,641 selected items in the materialization expansion were retained on
disk and passed full-content SHA-256 validation. Their native, exact-ten-second,
and unpadded up-to-sixty-second representations comprise 7,923 files and
83,210,585,185 bytes. This is a batch-specific storage total: archives, caches,
stems, older data, model weights and subsequent acquisitions are additional.
It is not a claim that the entire project occupies that amount of storage.

\subsection{Matched duration cohorts}

Equal60 v6 contains 2,174 exact-sixty-second recordings with native-stereo
provenance: 1,278 Human and 896 AI. Its Human sources are MTG-Jamendo (481),
MAESTRO (300), MedleyDB (156), MoisesDB (238), and Saraga (103). Its AI
sources are Suno (396) and Mureka (500). The cohort excludes the 33 native-mono
URMP recordings from the preceding v5 population. Mureka and Saraga had
already entered exploratory development; their use here is not a fresh
untouched confirmation.

Native30 contains 3,830 recordings, with 1,664 Human and 2,166 AI observations
from eleven sources (Table~\ref{tab:native30-roster}). It expands generator
coverage relative to Equal60, but remains restricted by duration and provenance
eligibility. The cohorts overlap, and their sizes are not added to the older
registry to manufacture an updated global census.

Native30 uses unpadded fixed excerpts and an explicit origin plan. A standardized
44.1-kHz file does not establish that the original recording possessed that
bandwidth: native sample-rate eligibility remains joined to original provenance.
Likewise, stereo measurements require meaningful native-stereo eligibility,
not duplicated mono channels. Internal mono 16-kHz analysis views used by some
measurements do not replace the stereo source needed by SC.

\subsection{Measurement availability is not cohort membership}

The committed Native30 FHSC measurement package retains all 3,830 rows.
Complete selected scalars are available on 3,819 rows for F, 3,789 for H,
and 3,829 for SC. F has 3,829 rows with status \texttt{ok}, but that status
does not imply that every one of its fifteen selected scalars is observed.
M is short-duration unavailable throughout Native30 and is not a primary
predictor there. Thus Equal60's eight families include M, whereas Native30's
seven-family base excludes M and its planned eighth family is BC.

The FHSC integrity audit verified 7,663 committed products, including 3,830
receipts and 3,830 SC frame archives. It established declared byte, schema,
lineage and missingness properties, not independent waveform re-extraction of
all features. Scientific missingness is retained rather than used to select
a more favorable cohort.
