\section{Frozen External Test Set: 500 Tracks per Non-Development Generator}
\label{sec:external-generator-500}

To test whether the applied filter transfers beyond Suno, HeartMuLa, and
ACE-Step, a new evaluation-only set was frozen from AIME
\citep{aime2025,aimehf2026}. AIME was selected because it provides Human and
generated music under the same 500 three-tag prompt conditions and exposes
exactly 500 outputs for every included generator. The source was pinned to main
revision
\hashvalue{b84d4be5eda830b6eb714998569dba73530f2601} and parquet revision
\hashvalue{1bdacac93127439e361bdd19d575d8b596bca4e3}. Neither detector scores nor
audio-derived features were used to choose membership.

\subsection{Generator and Leakage Boundaries}

The resulting manifest contains 5,000 AI tracks from ten generators, exactly
500 per generator, and 500 shared MTG-Jamendo Human controls. Suno v3, Suno
v3.5, generic Suno labels, HeartMuLa, and ACE-Step are hard exclusions. The
primary cohort comprises six generator families absent from the earlier
100-track AIME diagnostic; the secondary cohort expands four families that
appeared in that diagnostic. The exact 50 AI and 50 Human tracks previously
used are marked row by row and are not treated as pristine holdout examples.

All 500 prompt conditions were assigned with seed \texttt{20260904} to five
disjoint groups of 100. For every generator, a dedicated view contains its 500
AI tracks and the same 500 Human tracks; each of its five group manifests
contains 100 AI and 100 Human tracks with identical condition-ID sets. This
supports per-generator reporting and prompt-condition-paired bootstrap
intervals without selecting favorable random mixtures after evaluation.

\subsection{Standardisation and Native-Band Guard}

Every selected recording was converted to a deterministic 10.000 s crop at
44.1 kHz, stereo, signed 16-bit FLAC by \coderef{C33}. No zero-padding,
looping, loudness normalization, peak normalization, equalization, denoising,
or source separation was applied. Ten seconds is the longest no-padding
duration shared by all selected AIME systems; consequently, this set is
appropriate for frame-level spectral tests but not for long-horizon structural
claims.

Resampling does not create valid high-frequency evidence. The manifest therefore
records original sample rate, native Nyquist frequency, and per-band eligibility
for every track. The detector must mask features above the original Nyquist
limit rather than interpreting upsampled silence as an AI cue.

All 5,500 standardised files passed exhaustive decoding, duration, format,
path, and SHA-256 verification; there were 5,500 unique IDs, 5,500 unique output
hashes, no duplicate raw-audio hash groups, and zero verification errors. The
source audit \coderef{C34} covered 202 retained parquet shards totaling
53,386,356,734 bytes. Although retained boundary shards contained 500 Suno v3
and 260 Suno v3.5 rows, the allowlist rejected every one of them. The complete
protocol, source audit, manifests, checksum ledger, and execution logs are
indexed by \outref{O21}--\outref{O23}.

No detector score has yet been computed on this collection. The already frozen
detector and thresholds must first be evaluated on the six-model primary cohort,
with per-generator and pooled metrics reported. The four family-seen models must
then be reported separately, both including and excluding exact prior-diagnostic
overlaps. Any representation or threshold changed after seeing these results is
a new hypothesis and requires another untouched test set.
