# Early experiment input membership

This supplement preserves 1,420 input-membership rows from three historical
archive manifests: the 400-input initial frequency study, the 1,000-input
direct-band study, and 20 selected inputs for the stem pilot. The pilot candidate
pool is deliberately excluded. These are input memberships, not proof that all
requested measurements completed, and not 1,420 distinct songs.

The early frequency and selected stem-pilot relative source paths have no exact
matches in the direct-band manifest. Their legacy IDs also require separate
reconciliation with the later namespaced catalogues. Do not infer that all early
audio was covered by the later 1,000-input benchmark or its audio publication.
Conversely, path differences do not establish unique musical content.

The CSV preserves source locators, labels, duration, sample rate and crop start
as recorded historically. Original hashes and verified publication links remain
unresolved here. Source-member hashes in SUMMARY.json bind the source manifests;
the underlying historical archives have their separate preservation audit.

COMMIT.json binds inputs.csv and SUMMARY.json. This explanatory README was added
after that commitment and is not one of its bound generated products.
