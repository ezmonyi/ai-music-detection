# Saraga unchanged-original publication review

The official version-1.5 Zenodo page and pinned repository license were freshly
inspected on 2026-09-12. The repository explicitly covers audio under CC BY-NC
4.0. The historical Zenodo metadata reports CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Both allow
noncommercial redistribution with attribution; the differing ShareAlike clause
is not, by itself, a reason to indefinitely withhold unchanged originals.

Proposed next action: publish the 108 unchanged audited originals for this
noncommercial thesis archive, retaining both source license statements and
links without purporting to relicense the recordings. Preserve per-record
performer attribution and the distinction between 103 classifier members and
five additional recordings. Do not include transformed audio under this review.
No source files have been uploaded by this review document.

Before publication, revalidate the source hashes, bind public manifest rows to
the physical-source audit, preserve the official license text, and include an
explicit unchanged-audio notice. The source request to tell the maintainers
about research use has not been fulfilled; no email was sent on the user's behalf.
