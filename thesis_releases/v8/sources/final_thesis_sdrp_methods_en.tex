\section{Operational definitions of the original four families}
\label{sec:sdrp-methods}

The names S, D, R and P denote fixed measurement families, not four
independently validated authorship mechanisms. The expanded extractor uses
Demucs vocal estimates for S, the sum of bass/drums/other estimates for D,
retained beat/downbeat estimates for R, and retained All-In-One segment
boundaries combined with downbeats for P. Neural estimates are inputs to
deterministic measurements; they are not ground-truth events.

The retained neural front ends are Hybrid Transformer Demucs
\cite{rouard2022}, Beat This! \cite{foscarin2024}, and All-In-One metrical
and functional structure analysis \cite{kimnam2023}. The actual checkpoint
manifest resolves \texttt{htdemucs} to signature \texttt{955717e8},
Beat This! to \texttt{final0}, and \texttt{harmonix-all} to the ordered
eight-model ensemble \texttt{harmonix-fold0} through \texttt{harmonix-fold7}.
Thus the separator citation is for the Hybrid Transformer version, not
merely its earlier hybrid predecessor; \texttt{htdemucs\_ft} is not silently
substituted. The manifest at
\path{artifacts/source_diversity_expansion_20260905/results/inference_checkpoint_manifest.json}
retains the resolved file hashes. Native30 binds that manifest in its
inference contract. Published front-end benchmark scores do not establish
the accuracy of their event estimates on this project's generated music.

\subsection{S: bandwidth-controlled vocal spectral statistics}
The expanded implementation uses mono float64 vocal estimates with DC
removal, 44,100 Hz, 4,096-sample frames and hop 1,024. Its explicit
\texttt{numpy.hanning} window is symmetric; this differs from the periodic
Hann used in several other modules and must not be silently interchanged
when reproducing results. Power receives an epsilon of $10^{-14}$ before
the frozen external frequency-response correction. Active frames exceed
the larger of the 30th-percentile RMS level and the maximum minus 35 dB;
if fewer than 16 qualify, the strongest available frames are selected up
to 16. Consequently, frame selection alone does not certify audible vocals.
A separately retained vocal-activity sensitivity requires vocal-to-mix RMS
at least $-18$ dB, vocal RMS at least $-60$ dBFS and 16 active frames.

S8 denotes a common-band representation, not eight descriptors: it has
15 values and principally uses the 5--7.5 kHz interval, with lower-frequency
reference bands. S16 has 16 values, including a 12--20 kHz air-energy ratio,
and is a separate sensitivity representation. Original native sample-rate
eligibility is at least 16 kHz for S8 and 40 kHz for S16; resampling cannot
restore absent source bandwidth. The Native30 primary S family retains S8.

For corrected frame power $P_{k,t}$, high-band flatness is geometric mean
divided by arithmetic mean; normalized spectral entropy is
$-\sum_k p_{k,t}\log p_{k,t}/\log K$; crest is
the maximum-to-mean power ratio expressed in dB, with implementation
epsilons retained. Energy ratios compare high-band power with 1--5 kHz
body power and sibilance-band power with 0.3--5 kHz power. These frame
statistics are summarized by medians. Spectral slopes fit mean log power
against $\log_2(f)$ and therefore have units dB/octave.

The remaining descriptors capture spectral-envelope residual peaks and
periodicity, adjacent active-frame log-spectral cosine similarity and its
complement, high-band power standard deviation, a nominal 4--12 Hz
modulation share, sibilance contrast and burst rate. Residual peaks use
height 0.35, prominence 0.08 and minimum two-bin spacing after the fixed
lower-envelope normalization. Contrast is the 90th percentile minus median
of the sibilance ratio. Bursts exceed median plus 1.5 MAD; their rising-edge
count is divided by the selected-frame duration.

An important implementation limitation is that active frames are selected
\emph{before} adjacent-frame and modulation calculations. The latter use
the nominal hop-derived frame rate on this compressed active-frame sequence.
Thus, when activity contains gaps, adjacency need not mean neighbouring
waveform frames, and nominal modulation Hz and burst rates are not exact
continuous-time event measurements. These frozen descriptors are retained
for reproducibility, not relabelled as validated physical modulation rates.
The separate Q external assay must not be conflated with these S descriptors.

\subsection{D: event-conditioned accompaniment amplitude dispersion}
The accompaniment sum is converted to mono float32. A 4,096-point Hann
STFT with hop 512 and no boundary/tail padding supplies 30--8,000 Hz power.
Positive differences of $\log(1+10^6P)$, averaged across selected bins,
form the onset-strength sequence. Peaks must exceed median plus one MAD
and be at least approximately 80 ms apart.

For each candidate, the median power profile over 20--120 ms after the
STFT peak time is formed. Up to the 16 strongest local spectral maxima
contribute summed energy, expressed as $10\log_{10}(E+10^{-12})$.
With at least eight valid event amplitudes, D reports the 90th-minus-10th
percentile span, IQR, and median absolute adjacent-event change, all on
this logarithmic amplitude scale. Otherwise the values remain missing.
This is pseudo-velocity dispersion of an estimated accompaniment mixture,
not instrument-separated MIDI velocity or proof of MIDI control. Different
instruments, overlapping attacks, compression and separation errors can
alter the event distribution.

\subsection{R: beat-interval and local-tempo variation}
R requires at least 16 beat estimates and at least 15 positive finite
inter-beat intervals (IBIs). Despite its historical name \texttt{ibi\_cv},
the statistic is $\mathrm{IQR}(\mathrm{IBI})/\mathrm{median}(\mathrm{IBI})$,
\emph{not} the conventional standard-deviation coefficient of variation.
Instantaneous tempo is $60/\mathrm{IBI}$; values outside 30--300 BPM are
removed. With at least 14 remaining values, \texttt{tempo\_tv} is median
absolute adjacent tempo change divided by median tempo. Entropy uses
five-BPM histogram bins from 30 to 300 and normalization by the logarithm
of the full bin count, not only occupied bins.

Removing invalid intervals/tempos can create adjacency across an excluded
value; this retained statistic is a robust sequence descriptor rather than
an exact time-integrated tempo derivative. Empty valid beat output and
missing or malformed inference output have different provenance states.
Neither may be interpreted as an observed perfectly constant rhythm.

\subsection{P: segment-duration and downbeat-span proxies}
All-In-One segment starts and the final segment end are clipped to the
view, deduplicated and cleaned with a minimum one-second separation;
view endpoints zero and duration are included. At least three positive
segment durations are required for median duration, IQR/median and
normalized two-second-bin duration entropy. Histogram support is at least
32 seconds and expands for longer segments. The historical
\texttt{section\_duration\_cv} name again denotes IQR/median.

With at least four downbeats and four boundaries, boundaries are snapped
to nearest downbeat indices and deduplicated. Three positive index spans
are needed for median span, IQR/median and the fraction unequal to the
modal span; ties select the smallest modal span. These are estimated
downbeat spans, not annotated phrase lengths. Boundary snapping and
endpoint truncation may change them substantially, especially in short
views. The failed external P gate and missingness are reported separately.
