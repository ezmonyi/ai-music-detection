\begin{abstract}
This supplement records completed phase/group-delay, pitch-class and stereo
measurements for a fixed 3,830-recording cohort. It distinguishes successful
processing from complete feature availability and documents an independent
product-integrity audit. It contains no Native-30 classification accuracy:
the neural analysis and downstream single-family and combination evaluations
are not complete at this checkpoint. Paths below are relative to the
\path{artifacts/audio_phenomena_expansion_20260907/} experiment directory
unless explicitly absolute. This is a supplementary checkpoint, not the final
thesis or a claim of general AI attribution.
\end{abstract}
