\subsection{The complete exact-10-second candidate lattice}
The evaluation uses 8,361 development recordings: 2,161 Human recordings from
seven sources and 6,200 AI recordings from thirteen sources. It evaluates all
31 nonempty subsets of S/D/R/F/H at five training-group caps. P and M are not
made artificially observable by padding short clips. No historical locked or
pilot records enter model fitting. The independent audit passed all 21,380
checks, including 13,673,325 primary predictions and 211,575 metric cells;
missingness diagnostics were audited separately. All five raw prediction and
fold-registry files were copied locally, totaling 5,569,808,264 bytes, and their
full-file hashes matched the remote originals.

S+D at 0.632004/0.596233. This descriptive maximum is not a validated final

\subsection{Conditional gain is not source-composition robustness}
The uncertainty protocol was frozen after point estimates were available and
before resampling. It therefore is a post-point-estimate uncertainty analysis,
not a prospectively chosen discovery test. The previously established S+D
baseline is compared with +F, +H and +F+H at the all cap, with fitted models,
folds and thresholds unchanged. No multiplicity adjustment for the full
candidate lattice is claimed.

The 1,539 Human and 1,300 AI group sets overlap in 500 group identifiers,
giving 2,339 global groups. A shared 1,000-draw global-group bootstrap preserves
these cross-class/source dependencies. Separate 10,000-draw crossed-source
resampling varies the weights of the seven observed Human and thirteen
observed AI sources. It assesses observed-source composition sensitivity;
its percentile ranges are not future-generator confidence guarantees.

H and F+H have positive conditional intervals, but every source-composition
range crosses zero. Thus H supplies useful conditional information in this
corpus without establishing robust transfer across source mixtures. Four of
1,000 group draws contain undefined class-degenerate pair cells (104 recorded
undefined pair-metric rows); these are retained in the accounting, not replaced
with zeros. All 1,000 macro-delta draws remain finite. These intervals condition
on fitted models and do not include training-refit variability.

Independent reconstruction reproduced the saved point metrics to within
$2.23\times10^{-16}$ and paired deltas exactly. A separate root reconciliation
checked ten declared output hashes and 84 percentile/moment summaries with
maximum absolute discrepancy below $3.5\times10^{-18}$. Opposite H increments
between the 10- and 30-second studies do not identify a causal duration effect:
the cohorts and source compositions differ.

\subsection{Status of the matched native-60-second extension}
All 1,604 development input views have been materialized: 1,208 Human and
396 Suno recordings, partitioned into 67 inference shards. The 33,953,613,152
output bytes represent exact 60-second, 44.1-kHz stereo FLOAT WAV views of
the native intervals already used by F/H/M. Full-file materialization auditing
is a prerequisite to the separate RTX~5090 inference batch runner. Six-source
probe inference and unchanged old-family extraction have already passed strict
audio-frame, content-hash, beat, structure and spectrogram checks.

The planned seven-family evaluation retains all 1,604 rows for every one of
127 nonempty S/D/R/P/F/H/M subsets at caps 25, 50, 100, 200 and all. It also
reports 105 matched old-family-plus-new-family comparisons, including fifteen
M-only additions. Only Suno is a development AI source at this duration; source
holdout transfer and $J$ are therefore not reported for this study. Ordinary
group-CV performance must not be substituted for unseen-generator evidence.
No full seven-family numerical result is claimed at this checkpoint.
