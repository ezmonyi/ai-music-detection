\subsection{Coverage boundaries and local preservation}
Of 31,378 historical-hash-verified stem objects, 16,422 match the public snapshot
and 14,956 do not (50,814,442,064 unmatched bytes). A separate current-only
inventory contains 27,174 objects: two match and 27,172 do not
(68,652,547,568 unmatched bytes). These scopes overlap and must not be summed.
Current file hashes cannot retroactively establish which bytes an earlier
experiment read. There are also 126 unanchored historical references; these are
not established missing-file findings. Snapshot matching does not inspect
archive contents or private backups and does not grant redistribution rights.

The frozen 14,244-object processed-input scope has preservation coverage through
966 older public-snapshot matches, 8,794 accepted public supplement objects and
4,484 locally archived rights-held objects. The local TAR is 9,217,576,960 bytes;
all 4,484 audio members passed streaming SHA-256 verification. It is not publicly
released. This closes that preservation scope only, not whole-project delivery.
Other unresolved source licenses and historical input scopes remain open.
