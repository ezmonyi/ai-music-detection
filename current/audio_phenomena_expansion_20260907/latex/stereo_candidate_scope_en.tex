\section{Prospective extension: native stereo relationships}
\label{sec:stereo-candidate-scope}

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
Inter-channel level, phase and coherence describe a production phenomenon
distinct from within-channel high-frequency energy. Parametric stereo research
provides a measurement basis, but allows multiple plausible spatial renditions
of the same music
(\href{https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2023/paper/000035.pdf}{Serr\`a et al., ISMIR 2023}).
The music-reconstruction study
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14912v2}{\emph{Back to Ear}, arXiv:2509.14912v2}
measures cross-channel phase preservation relative to an original reference.
That reference-dependent CCPC must not be relabelled as a blind AI/Human score.
The proposal below instead measures internal channel relationships; no
attribution direction is assumed. This is not an admitted eighth family and
does not change the frozen seven-family v5 evaluation.

\paragraph{Candidate observables.}
For bandwise STFT coefficients, define
\[
 E_L=\sum_f |L_f|^2,\quad E_R=\sum_f |R_f|^2,\quad
 C=\sum_f L_f\overline{R_f}.
\]
Proposed observations are $10\log_{10}(E_L/E_R)$,
$\operatorname{Re}(C)/\sqrt{E_LE_R}$,
$|C|/\sqrt{E_LE_R}$, side-energy fraction, and circular adjacent-frame
changes of $\arg C$. Only active bands are valid; phase changes additionally
require reliable cross-energy at both adjacent frames. Missing frames cannot
be bridged. The prospective implementation uses 44.1 kHz, a 4,096-sample
periodic Hann window, hop 1,024, no padding, and five fixed bands spanning
80--20,000 Hz. These coarse bands are our adaptation, not a reproduction of
the cited paper's band system.
For the 50 candidate scalar descriptors, IID and wrapped IPD increments are
converted to absolute values before median/IQR aggregation. This preserves
channel-swap invariance and avoids applying linear quantiles across the
signed-angle branch cut; the signed per-frame arrays remain available.
These absolute summaries do not preserve panning direction or temporal order.

\paragraph{Identifiability and controls.}
The magnitude of a single-bin normalized cross-spectrum is identically one
when both coefficients are nonzero; aggregation must precede normalization's
final magnitude interpretation. Duplicated mono and anti-phase stereo both
have magnitude coherence one but opposite signed coherence. Moreover,
the side fraction satisfies
\[
 q_S=\frac12-\frac{\operatorname{Re}C}{E_L+E_R},
\]
so these descriptors are not independent corroborating phenomena. Native
channel and bandwidth provenance must be retained: duplicating mono during
standardization cannot manufacture valid spatial evidence. Artistic panning,
chorus, doubling, reverberation and codec processing are necessary controls.

\paragraph{Admission status and reproducibility.}
The stored-media inventory found 33 original mono URMP recordings despite
two-channel standardized outputs. A native-channel gate would remove that
entire Human source. The other 2,174 recordings (1,278 Human and 896 AI) have
recorded original two-channel containers; this is not yet acoustic-stereo
or bandwidth validation. A future spatial-feature comparison must rerun its
old-feature baseline on exactly the same eligible rows/groups, rather than
attribute a population-change effect to the feature. See
\path{artifacts/audio_phenomena_expansion_20260907/V5_CHANNEL_PROVENANCE_INVENTORY_EN.md}
and its standard-library reproduction helper
\path{artifacts/audio_phenomena_expansion_20260907/code/summarize_v5_channel_provenance.py}.

At this checkpoint, 14 mathematical/synthetic tests passed parent replay both
locally and on the existing 5090-5 CPU scientific runtime, with warnings treated
as errors. Code review corrected circular-summary, short-frame-status and
finite-overflow issues. The review record is
\path{artifacts/audio_phenomena_expansion_20260907/audit/stereo_candidate_v1_parent_review.json}.
No external musical validation or detection gain is claimed. Admission first
requires a separately frozen external music corpus with paired known spatial
manipulations and unchanged controls, group-aware parameter-recovery tests,
and codec/bandwidth stress tests. Only a subsequent, separately frozen
exploratory experiment could compare this candidate alone and in combinations.
Source-reused evaluations remain development results.
