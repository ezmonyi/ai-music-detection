\subsection{A separate VocalSet vibrato-presence assay}
\label{sec:vocalset-presence-dev}

The failed MIR-1K rate/extent gate was preserved. A separate, preregistered
development assay instead tested instructed clip-level straight-tone versus
vibrato conditions in existing original VocalSet recordings. The metadata-only
pairing yielded 90 pairs, allocated by singer into 43 development and 47 held-out
pairs. The archive member with conflicting m9/male8 identity was excluded.
Matched exercise/vowel identity does not guarantee identical performed pitch,
and the original breath-annotation-selected subset is not a random VocalSet
sample. Only the development recordings were opened for this assay.

The primary descriptor was the fraction of independently eligible stable-F0
windows exhibiting periodic modulation in the fixed 4--8-Hz range. Adequately
observed straight-tone absence could be zero; insufficient stable pitch remained
missing. The unchanged coarse pYIN configuration used 16~kHz, frame length
1024, hop length 160 and C2--C6. Mono 44.1-kHz sources were resampled with a
Kaiser-5 polyphase filter without gain normalization or DC removal. The
development-only runner froze source, code, configuration and runtime hashes
before scoring; twelve unit tests passed.

The development gate failed. Although 33 of 37 observable pairs had the expected
direction, systematically losing more vibrato recordings could bias conditional
comparisons. No threshold was relaxed, missing pairs were not removed from
accounting, and the 47 held-out pairs remained unopened. The result does not
validate continuous rate/extent, separated-stem robustness, or AI/Human utility;
V was not admitted as a classifier feature. Independent reconciliation passed
883 artifact, source/contour, window-accounting and gate checks without refitting
or rerunning pitch inference.
