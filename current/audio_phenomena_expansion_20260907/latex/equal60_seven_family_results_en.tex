\subsection{Seven-family evaluation on matched native sixty-second intervals}

After complete neural-output validation, unchanged four-family extraction,
recovery-provenance review and independent preregistration freezing, we evaluated
all 127 nonempty S/D/R/P/F/H/M combinations at five training-size caps and five
shared global-group folds. The cohort contains 1,604 development recordings
(1,208 Human, 396 Suno; 1,274 global groups). The 438 locked and 50 pilot rows,
and the separately reserved Mureka500 cohort, do not enter fitting. Each family
covers the same native 60s interval, but retains its specified measurement front
end. All 54 numeric descriptors and all unavailable observations are retained.
Weighted ridge uses penalty 10 and fixed threshold 0.5, with training-only
imputation and scaling. The caps 25/50/100/200/all are maximum training groups
per source within each fold, not song counts. The corresponding training-row
ranges across folds are 240--273, 497--565, 665--738, 955--1,023 and
1,250--1,328. The same five test folds contain 354, 276, 334, 297 and 343
recordings under every cap and combination. No winner is selected.

Our headline statistic uniformly averages five Human-source/Suno pairs in each
of five folds. At all-cap, S/D/R/P yields AUC0.906513 and balanced accuracy
0.843986. Adding phase-evolution family F yields0.936725/0.870106, a BA increment
of2.612 percentage points. Adding all F/H/M yields0.943630/0.878733, a BA
increment of3.475 percentage points. Adding H alone yields0.908258/0.844647;
adding M alone yields0.910354/0.843268. These are descriptive paired point
contrasts, not significance tests or established rankings of causal mechanisms.
At the smallest cap, all seven families yield0.894733/0.824131, below
S/D/R/P/F at0.905717/0.840459; adding more descriptors is not uniformly useful.

The companion statistic pools all Human rows within each fold before computing
and averaging its metric. It gives0.864949/0.803214 for S/D/R/P,
0.904392/0.830497 for S/D/R/P/F and0.917163/0.844176 for all seven.
These two aggregation conventions must not be conflated. For example,
MTG-Jamendo versus Suno has seven-family AUC0.848091 and BA0.758256, with
Human specificity0.645060, much lower than the equal-source aggregate.
URMP contributes only33 recordings, including two test rows in one fold.
Suno is the sole development AI source, so source-transfer J is undefined and
none of these results establishes unseen-generator performance.

Missingness diagnostics qualify the structure proxy P. Its all-cap AUC/BA
is0.695069/0.650655 with values plus missing indicators,
0.642236/0.615334 with median-imputed values alone, and0.576730/0.579173
with missing indicators alone. P-duration columns are missing on265 rows and
P-bar columns on300; R has at least one missing descriptor on nine rows,
including five with all three missing. The other four retain IBI CV and are
not removed by an eligibility filter. The F increment survives
removal of explicit missing indicators: old-four AUC0.905134 increases to
0.936692 with F. This does not eliminate genre, instrumentation, codec or
production-chain confounding. Prior external controls already showed that F
is sensitive to separation and encoding. H is a pitch-class path proxy, not
chord transcription, and M is a recurrence proxy, not validated natural-motif
detection.
