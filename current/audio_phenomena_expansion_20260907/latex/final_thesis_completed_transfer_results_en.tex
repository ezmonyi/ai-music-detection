\section{Completed development experiments and transfer boundaries}
\label{sec:completed-transfer-results}

This section reports completed experiments rather than pending Native30 fits.
The exact-ten-second and exact-sixty-second cohorts differ; their scores are
not a controlled comparison of listening duration. All results retain their
original development status. In particular, inspecting many feature subsets
does not create an untouched validation set for the selected maximum.

\subsection{Exact10: conditional tonal-path gains}

The five-family S/D/R/F/H experiment evaluated 31 nonempty subsets at five
training caps on 8,361 development recordings: 2,161 Human recordings from
seven sources and 6,200 AI recordings from thirteen sources. P was unavailable
at ten seconds and was not included. At the all-data cap, S+D had
$J=0.632004$ and mean BA $=0.596233$; S+D+H reached $J=0.660124$ and mean
BA $=0.616698$. These are the finalized Exact10 values, distinct from the
slightly different historical four-family evaluation.

The analysis used 1,000 paired global-group draws and 10,000 separate
crossed-source draws, retaining the fitted models and threshold. Because 500
groups crossed labels, the union contained 2,339 groups, not the sum of the
within-class group counts. Four group draws generated 104 explicitly undefined
pair cells; none was replaced by a chance score. All aggregate differences
remained finite under the declared aggregation policy.

The H and F+H conditional group intervals were positive, but every source
composition range crossed zero (Table~\ref{tab:exact10-conditional}). Thus the
evidence supports a conditional improvement on these observations, not stable
improvement under arbitrary source shift. The uncertainty protocol was frozen
after point estimates were visible and is not a multiplicity-adjusted discovery
claim. The classifier audit passed 21,380 checks; a separate saved-output
verification passed 78,111 assertions. These audits establish specified
numerical and accounting properties, not universal detection validity.

\subsection{Equal60 v6: stereo is not uniformly additive}

The native-stereo, exact-sixty-second cohort contained 2,174 recordings:
1,278 Human and 896 AI. Human sources were MTG-Jamendo (481), MAESTRO (300),
MedleyDB (156), MoisesDB (238), and Saraga (103). AI sources were Suno (396)
and Mureka (500). Mureka and Saraga had already participated in exploratory
development. Each generator-held-out arm trained on the other generator;
this is not an external test on a third generator.

Eight families supplied 60 descriptors: S (15), D (3), R (3), P (6), F (15),
H (6), M (6), and SC (6). Neither BC nor failed modulation family Q was
included. All 255 nonempty subsets and five caps were evaluated under 38
valid folds: 23 Human-source, ten generator, and five ordinary-group folds.
The two empty Saraga fold cells remained omitted. Comparisons below are within
v6; they do not subtract scores from the different v5 population.

SC alone had generator-held-out BA of 71.88\%, yet adding it to Seven
decreased that endpoint from 57.32\% to 56.51\%. Ordinary-group BA increased
from 88.67\% to 89.66\%. A standalone association is therefore not sufficient
to predict a beneficial multivariate increment. Across all 127 matched SC
additions at the all cap, generator BA increased for 64 subsets and decreased
for 63. These correlated descriptive counts are not independent trials or
significance tests.

\subsection{Errors concealed by an ordinary-group summary}

Under Eight, held-out Mureka sensitivity was 136/500 (27.20\%) and held-out
Suno sensitivity was 63/396 (15.91\%). Human specificity was 41/103 (39.81\%)
on Saraga and 273/481 (56.76\%) on MTG-Jamendo. These rates belong to separate
fitted held-source arms and cannot be combined into one confusion matrix.
They demonstrate why the 89.66\% ordinary-group BA cannot serve as a claim of
reliable unseen-generator attribution.

SC missingness-only AUC and BA were 0.5 in all three directions, while its
values-only results equalled its primary results. This excludes one specific
missingness explanation for SC within this cohort, but not genre, panning,
mastering, codec, or source effects. Eight's generator-held-out BA was 56.26\%
in the values-only diagnostic and 56.32\% in the missingness-only diagnostic,
versus 56.51\% in the primary representation. These are separately fitted
diagnostics, not an additive decomposition of the primary score.

The Equal60 numerical audit covered 67,830 models, 19,624,290 score rows and
60,690 OOF endpoint cells. Maximum affine-score replay discrepancy was
$3.997\times10^{-15}$. Checks of training transformations, identities,
denominators, and normal-equation residuals support numerical consistency;
stationarity checks do not independently reproduce an optimizer or establish
causal authorship attribution.
