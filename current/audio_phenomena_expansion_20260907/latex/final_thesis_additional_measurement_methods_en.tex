\section{Additional deterministic measurement definitions}
\label{sec:additional-measurements}

The additional families are operational descriptors, not interchangeable
definitions of a generation artifact. Their scalar catalogues are fixed
separately from quality fields and diagnostic arrays. A missing measurement
is not a measured absence of the phenomenon. Native30 uses 15 F, six H and
six SC descriptors in its 54-column seven-family representation; the BC
extension appends exactly one scalar. Equal60 additionally uses six M
descriptors. The following definitions describe retained implementations,
not a claim that pending Native30 classification has completed.

\subsection{F: phase residuals and local group-delay dispersion}
For STFT phase $\phi_{k,t}$, frequency $f_k$, sample rate $f_s$ and hop $h$,
the phase residual is
\begin{equation}
 r_{k,t}=\operatorname{wrap}\left(\phi_{k,t+1}-\phi_{k,t}
                 -2\pi f_kh/f_s\right).
\end{equation}
With valid-pair weights $w_{k,t}=\sqrt{|X_{k,t+1}||X_{k,t}|}$,
the reported circular variance is
\begin{equation}
 V_r=1-\frac{\left|\sum_{(k,t)\in\mathcal V}
                     w_{k,t}e^{\mathrm{i}r_{k,t}}\right|}
                    {\sum_{(k,t)\in\mathcal V}w_{k,t}}.
\end{equation}
Aggregation is joint across valid time--frequency pairs, not a mean of
independent per-bin circular variances. The expected rotation has the
forward-FFT sign convention used by the implementation.

At 16 kHz the window is 1,024 samples (64 ms), with a 256-sample hop,
periodic Hann weighting, no STFT boundary extension and no tail padding.
The module subtracts the waveform mean. Its absolute waveform RMS floor
is $-100$ dBFS. Bin magnitude must exceed both the frame maximum minus
40 dB and the excerpt maximum minus 70 dB; frequencies are restricted to
50 Hz through 95\% of Nyquist. Active frames use a separate relative
frame-power rule: the peak minus 50 dB when peak-to-20th-percentile spread
is below 12 dB, otherwise the peak minus 45 dB.

The implemented local delay proxy is
\begin{equation}
 \tau_{k,t}=-\frac{\operatorname{wrap}(\phi_{k+1,t}-\phi_{k,t})}
                         {2\pi/N}\frac{1000}{f_s}\quad\text{ms}.
\end{equation}
It is a wrapped adjacent-bin phase-difference proxy, not an unwrapped
physical propagation-delay estimator. Reliable adjacent bins are required.
Within each frame, the median of within-band delay IQRs and the IQR of
band-median delays are computed. The bands at 16 kHz are 50--500,
500--2,000, 2,000--4,000 and 4,000--7,600 Hz. A band needs at least three
valid delay values; cross-band dispersion needs two band centres.

All-frame, attack, sustain and decay summaries are retained. Regions are
assigned from the slope of a three-frame-smoothed log-energy sequence,
using its active-frame median and a threshold of
$\max(0.5,2.5\,\mathrm{MAD})$. These are energy-direction regions, not
annotated instrument envelopes. The all-frame region requires eight active
frames and other regions require six; circular variance additionally
requires 16 valid pairs. Delay summaries need at least
$\max(3,\lfloor n_{\mathrm{region}}/4\rfloor)$ valid frame values.
Three decay-minus-sustain contrasts complete the 15 features.

\subsection{H and M: chroma paths and bounded-lag recurrence}
The chroma implementation uses a 4,096-point Hann STFT at 16 kHz, a
1,024-sample hop, no centring, power input and fixed tuning zero. Frames
are active above 1\% of the maximum spectral RMS. L1-normalized chroma
must have normalized entropy at most 0.92 to be informative. One-second
blocks average informative chroma frames and are then L2-normalized;
each block needs at least two informative frames and at least 25\% of the
rounded expected frame count.

H requires at least eight seconds and the implementation's block/path
coverage checks. Its six descriptors are normalized aggregate pitch-class
entropy, dominant-token entropy, median and IQR of adjacent-block cosine
distance, median circular dominant-token step, and the rate of large steps.
For dominant pitch-class tokens $a,b\in\{0,\ldots,11\}$ the step is
$\min(|a-b|,12-|a-b|)/6$; a large step is at least 0.5. Only adjacent
eligible blocks are compared: a gap is not bridged to create a transition.
These quantities do not constitute chord transcription or harmonic quality.

M requires at least 45 seconds. Four-second chroma patterns are compared
at integer lags from eight seconds through
$\min(60,T-4)$, allowing all 12 pitch-class rotations. The implementation
does not build a full self-similarity matrix. For each lag it retains the
90th-percentile similarity and the fraction at or above 0.88. It selects
an early sufficiently supported lag within 0.01 of the global peak, with
the specified density safeguard. The six outputs describe peak similarity,
density, lag contrast, selected lag, transposition and returning-pattern
count. They are not independent motif events. Native30 cannot meet the
45-second requirement, so M is not a Native30 primary family.

\subsection{SC: retained stereo descriptors}
The primary SC catalogue selects three bands, 80--500, 500--2,000 and
2,000--6,000 Hz, with two descriptors per band: median absolute
interchannel level difference and median side-energy fraction. For complex
band coefficients $L$ and $R$, mid and side are $(L+R)/2$ and $(L-R)/2$;
the latter descriptor is
\begin{equation}
 q_{\mathrm{side}}=
 \frac{\sum_k |(L_k-R_k)/2|^2}
 {\sum_k |(L_k+R_k)/2|^2+\sum_k |(L_k-R_k)/2|^2}.
\end{equation}
This fraction lies in $[0,1]$ on eligible frames. Silent bands are masked;
interchannel level difference additionally requires sufficient energy in
both channels. Full coherence, interchannel-phase and five-band arrays
remain diagnostic evidence, not extra primary predictors. These measures
may capture panning and mastering conventions; they are not intrinsic
authorship signatures.

\subsection{BC: fixed-triplet squared bicoherence}
The fixed target is $(f_1,f_2,f_1+f_2)=(500,750,1250)$ Hz. Within one
four-second pool, let $A_k=X_k(32)X_k(48)$ and $C_k=X_k(80)$. Then
\begin{equation}
 b^2=\frac{|\sum_k A_k\overline{C_k}|^2}
                 {(\sum_k|A_k|^2)(\sum_k|C_k|^2)}.
\end{equation}
There are 247 STFT frames per pool: 1,024-point periodic Hann, hop 256,
per-frame mean subtraction, and FFT normalization by the window sum.
No frame crosses a pool boundary. Each target bin must have pooled energy
at least $10^{-6}$ of the total non-DC coefficient energy. The recording
scalar is the median of eligible pool values, or null if neither pool is
eligible. Overlapping STFT frames are not independent observations.

For the frozen Native30 transfer view, decoded stereo FLOAT samples are
promoted to float64 and arithmetically averaged. The entire 30 seconds is
resampled from 44,100 to 16,000 Hz using a 160/441 polyphase ratio,
Kaiser parameter 5 and constant-zero padding. Only then is the centre
eight seconds selected, samples $[176000,304000)$ of the 480,000-sample
view, followed by fixed gain 0.25. The two pools therefore cover seconds
$[11,15)$ and $[15,19)$ of the Native30 input. There is no global DC
subtraction, channel selection, normalization or fallback for cancellation.
This view intentionally differs from the F/H mono loader. Full 228-cell
arrays support replay, but the fixed target is the sole added predictor.

High bicoherence need not imply nonlinear generation: stable linear tones
can also yield high values, and this statistic is amplitude-weighted rather
than phase-only. A controlled external gate therefore motivates a fixed
measurement transfer, not a claim about an AI generation mechanism.
