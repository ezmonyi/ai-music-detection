\section{Completed frozen-model transfer on YuE2 Native60}
\label{sec:yue2-native60}
Of the 500 original YuE2 outputs, 276 support an exact native center-60-second
interval: 223 development and 53 locked recordings. The remaining 224 are
excluded by duration, not padded. Every crop was checked against the original
48-kHz stereo audio. Neural front ends use explicitly resampled 44.1-kHz views;
F/H/M extraction uses the historical mono 16-kHz analysis loader. These are
different documented views of the same interval, not interchangeable files.

All 276 neural and SDRP outputs were completed and hash-verified. Repeating the
original SDRP extractor produced exactly matching descriptor values on all
records; F/H/M values were reconciled with their committed per-record outputs.
This checks reproducibility and record consistency, not a separately implemented
acoustic algorithm. F/H/M audio extraction was not repeated in that final audit.

The historical equal60 model publication was independently re-audited; its
1,604-recording development population has no ID or group overlap with these
YuE2 records. The transfer applies 127 subsets at five original caps and five
stored folds (3,175 model applications), without fitting or threshold changes.
The historical families are S/D/R/P/F/H/M, not the newer SC/BC extension.
The 63 non-M subsets are reported separately from 64 M-containing subsets,
which remain secondary historical diagnostics. M is not thereby admitted as a
validated general detector family.

All 876,300 per-record predictions were checked against a second, scalar
implementation of the saved imputation, missing indicators, scaling and linear
coefficients. Maximum score discrepancy was $2.22\times10^{-15}$ and all
threshold decisions agreed. Persisted predictions were then independently
counted and matched to all 9,525 per-model/population summaries. Repetition
across models does not create 876,300 independent audio samples.

The locked-population all-cap mean sensitivity is 91.6981\% for S/D/R/P,
97.3585\% for S/D/R/P/F/H and 92.4528\% for S/D/R/P/F/H/M. S alone reaches
98.1132\%. These observations do not establish a superior binary classifier:
human specificity, balanced accuracy and ROC AUC are not estimable from an
AI-only population. The corresponding all-eligible sensitivities are 91.9565\%,
96.0870\%, 94.2029\% and 97.3913\%. All caps and subsets, including low
single-family sensitivities, remain available in the full table; no winner is
selected from this transfer set.

Length eligibility selects only 55.2\% of the generated corpus and can correlate
with musical structure. The 53 locked records are a subset of the earlier
100-recording locked population, not a newly acquired independent test set.
Earlier Native30 and legacy analyses preceded this transfer. Differences from
Native30 cannot be attributed solely to duration, because population selection,
historical model training and feature definitions also differ.
