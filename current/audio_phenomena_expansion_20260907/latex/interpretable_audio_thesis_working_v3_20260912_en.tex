\section{Interim synthesis and remaining limitations}
The completed experiments show that interpretable measurements can provide
label-associated information without uniquely identifying authorship. The
Native30 results reinforce a central generalization limit: increasing feature
count or training quantity does not guarantee better held-generator decisions.
External measurement validation, source-held-out classification, and faithful
reproduction are separate requirements. No descriptor family should be accepted
as a causal AI signature merely because it improves one fitted classifier.

Native-channel and duration eligibility select particular populations. Source
labels incompletely represent genre, language, culture, instrumentation, editing,
codec, and mastering practice. Pretrained separators and music-understanding
front ends add their own domain sensitivity. Human-produced music may use MIDI,
synthesis, and extensive processing. These detectors should not be used as sole
evidence to penalize a musician or certify a recording.

The final synthesis must incorporate the pending BC and YuE2 evaluations rather
than extrapolate from their intermediate measurements. The YuE2 extension has
398 eligible development recordings and 100 separately retained locked-test
recordings; two shorter originals are excluded from Native30 rather than padded.
Shared prompt dependencies must remain excluded across training and evaluation.
